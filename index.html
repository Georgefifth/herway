    How HerWay scores a route

    Every street segment gets a Safety Score (0–100) from transparent factors — no black box:

    • 🔦 Street lighting — matters most after dark
    • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Foot traffic — busy streets deter harassment
    • 🏪 Open businesses — “eyes on the street”, open-late weighted at night
    • ⚠️ Incident reports — community reports push scores down
    • 🛟 Safe havens — police, hospitals, 24h stores nearby push scores up

    Move the time-of-day slider and watch the same street change score — a well-lit plaza at noon can be a risk at 2am.

    Demo build: factors come from a procedural model + your reports. Production would fuse OpenStreetMap, municipal open data and live community reports.

    Why HerWay exists

    9 in 10 women in cities worldwide report experiencing street harassment — and most say it shapes which routes they walk, when they leave, and whether they go at all.

    HerWay turns scattered community knowledge into a routing decision: not the fastest way home — the safest one.

    • SDG 5 — Gender Equality: freedom of movement without fear is a prerequisite for equal access to education, work and public life.
    • SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities: crowdsourced reports surface exactly where lighting, policing and urban design fail — data cities can act on.
